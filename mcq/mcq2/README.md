# MCQ 2

**Worth 32% of the module.** Held in person during the lab slot. No lecture
and no lab that week, and it is the last week of the module.

## What it covers

Everything taught after the reading week: security of AI-generated code,
CLI coding agents, CI/CD and evals, and vibe coding against spec-driven
development — the lectures and the labs both.

## How to prepare

- Re-read the decks on the
  [module site](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/).
- Work back through the labs — the exercises are where most questions
  come from.
- Read the worked feedback on the Practical Assessments you have done; the
  MCQ draws on the same labs.
- Use the [practice app](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/practice/)
  in exam mode.
