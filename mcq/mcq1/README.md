# MCQ 1

**Worth 32% of the module.** Held in person during the lab slot. No lecture
and no lab that week.

## What it covers

Everything taught before the reading week: the module introduction and
overview, prompting and context engineering, retrieval and grounding, MCP,
and coding agents — the lectures and the labs both. If it was in a deck or
in a lab README, it is examinable.

## How to prepare

- The reading week sits immediately before this MCQ, deliberately. Use it
  to finish any lab you did not complete — every lab before the break is
  examinable.
- Re-read the decks on the
  [module site](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/).
- Work back through the labs you did — the exercises are where most
  questions come from.
- Read the worked feedback on the Practical Assessments you have done. Each
  shows it once its week has closed, and the MCQ draws on the same labs.
- Use the [practice app](https://danielcregg.is-a.dev/ai-assisted-programming/practice/)
  in exam mode: same length and timing as the real thing.

## Format

Multiple choice, sat in the lab under exam conditions.
